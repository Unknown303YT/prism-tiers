# PrismTiers Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PrismTiers ("the Bot") collects, uses, and stores information when you use its services.

By using PrismTiers, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

PrismTiers may collect and store the following information.

### Discord Information

- Discord User ID
- Discord username and display name
- Discord server IDs
- Discord role and permission information

### Tiering Information

- Minecraft usernames
- Tier rankings
- Test results
- Test history
- Applications
- Reviewer notes
- Tier-related moderation records

### Technical Information

- Bot configuration data
- Server settings
- Command usage data
- Error logs
- System performance information

---

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected information may be used to:

- Provide PrismTiers features
- Store and display tier information
- Process applications and tests
- Maintain bot functionality
- Improve reliability and security
- Prevent abuse

We do not sell user information.

---

## 4. Data Storage

PrismTiers may store information using third-party infrastructure providers, including:

- Supabase databases
- Redis databases
- Hosting providers

Data may be processed or stored by these providers according to their own policies.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally share personal information with third parties except when:

- Required to operate PrismTiers
- Required by law
- Necessary to protect the security of PrismTiers or its users

Server administrators may be able to view information generated within their server.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide PrismTiers services.

Some information, such as historical tier results, may be retained to maintain ranking history and community records.

---

## 7. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data, including:

- Requesting access to stored information
- Requesting correction of inaccurate information
- Requesting deletion of certain information

Requests can be made through PrismTiers support channels.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

PrismTiers is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements.

We do not knowingly collect information from users below Discord's minimum required age.

---

## 9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information, including:

- Restricting access to databases
- Using secure authentication methods
- Protecting sensitive credentials

However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

---

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Continued use of PrismTiers after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.

---

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the PrismTiers development team through the official support channels.
